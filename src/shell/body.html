Algorithm testdev only

Hides the last fifth of a swipe session, rebuilds the taste model from the rest, then checks whether the pieces you actually liked come out on top. Gives every change a number instead of a feeling.

Ready.

How to read it. AUC is the headline: take one piece you liked and one you skipped, and it's how often the model ranks the liked one higher. 0.50 is a coin flip, 0.70 is decent, 0.80+ is strong. P@10 is how many of its top 10 picks you actually liked. The two grey rows are deliberately dumb baselines — if the real algorithm can't beat them, it isn't earning its keep.
